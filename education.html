Education at Macleans

We have three main cornerstones at Macleans College that are guiding principles and values:

  • Whānau house system
  • Quality education and academic excellence
  • Extra-curricular activities and participation

Whānau houses and form classes

Macleans has 8 whānau houses: Batten, Hillary, Kupe, Mansfield, Rutherford, Snell, Te Kanawa and Upham. Each student belongs to a form class in their year level within a house. The house system allows students to feel acknowledged in a large school of over 3000 students.

Form classes are a smaller group of students who meet with their form teacher in a classroom each morning for half an hour, and often take classes together for most subjects in their junior years.

Periods and school days

The school day is divided into 5 periods. Students follow a fortnightly timetable and go to different classes for their subjects, with morning tea and lunch breaks during the day. The schedule can change, so check the school's bell times for the more details.

Extra-curricular activities

Students can get involved in extra-curriculars too. There are sports, performing arts, and academic groups and clubs.
