Academic courses

At Macleans, students study a range of subjects and can branch into more learning pathways in their senior years. Here is a general introduction; details may change, for instance due to NCEA's changes or school decisions on extension classes.

Junior students

Everyone must take 4 core subjects, each of which is taken 4 days a week:

  • MAT - Mathematics
  • ENG - English
  • SCI - Science
  • SOS - Social studies

Additionally, students may choose 4 option subjects to take in the whole year. 2 are taken in each semester, and 3 days a week. Here are some examples:

  • DCT - Digital Computer Technology
  • COM - Commerce
  • PRM - Product Materials
  • ART - Art
  • MUS - Music
  • ... And many more!

Learning pathways

In Years 9 and 10, students build a broad foundation. In the senior years, Macleans offers NCEA and Cambridge International qualification pathways.

Extension classes

Extension classes allow students take on accelerated work. Under the school's programme from 2026, there is no traditional "A class", so all classes are mixed ability. Students sit a maths test early in Term 1 and may be invited into an accelerated maths class: 9MAA. Other core subjects are still required to be taken with a student's form class. Additional English and Science extension classes may begin in Year 10, however there is still some uncertainty.

For the latest details, see the school's extension studies page.
